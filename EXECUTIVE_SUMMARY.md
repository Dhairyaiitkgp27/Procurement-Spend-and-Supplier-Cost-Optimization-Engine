# Executive Summary — Procurement Cost Optimization

*Prepared as a management-consulting style briefing. Procurement Spend & Supplier Cost Optimization Engine.*

> **Data disclaimer.** The dataset in this project is **synthetic**, generated with a fixed random seed to be realistic and fully reproducible. It does **not** represent any real company. All savings figures are **modeled / estimated opportunity**, not realized savings.

## The situation

A multinational's procurement organization spends **$692.8M** across
**106 suppliers**, **10 categories** and
**5 business units** (~3.49 years of PO data,
46,424 lines). The spend base is only moderately consolidated
(43 suppliers cover 80% of spend) and contract
discipline is weak: **only 53% of spend is on-contract**
against a 90% target.

## The opportunity (modeled)

Benchmarking every supplier price within its (item, region cost-tier) peer group
surfaces a **$92.7M gross savings gap**. At a realistic
**50% capture**, that is **$46.4M of modeled savings —
6.69% of spend (~$13.3M/yr)**,
with a governance range of **$15.5M → $44.4M → $67.5M**
across conservative, base and aggressive scenarios. A further
**$1.1M/yr** of financial value sits in payment-term
optimization.

The single largest lever is **contract compliance**: redirecting maverick,
off-contract spend to negotiated prices is worth
$51.8M
gross on its own.

## Where to act first

Top negotiation targets (full list in `RESULTS.md`):

- **Keystone Components** (Facilities) — $39.8M spend, 25% above benchmark, ~$6.3M modeled savings → **NEGOTIATE**
- **Atlas Solutions** (Raw_Metals) — $56.6M spend, 10% above benchmark, ~$4.5M modeled savings → **REPLACE**
- **Cascade Group** (Logistics_Freight) — $18.8M spend, 41% above benchmark, ~$3.5M modeled savings → **REPLACE**
- **Vertex Solutions** (IT_Software) — $14.2M spend, 22% above benchmark, ~$1.8M modeled savings → **REPLACE**
- **Quantum Global** (Logistics_Freight) — $10.1M spend, 20% above benchmark, ~$1.3M modeled savings → **REPLACE**

## Recommended program

1. **Compliance first (highest ROI, lowest risk).** Route off-contract spend onto
   existing contracts and reference prices — the biggest, lowest-effort lever.
2. **Renegotiate the persistent overpayers.** 28 suppliers
   are structurally above market; the Top-10 target list sequences them by value and negotiability.
3. **Consolidate fragmented categories** toward best-in-class suppliers where quality is comparable.
4. **De-risk the tail.** 20 suppliers are high/critical
   risk ($184.0M of spend); 2 single-source
   items ($58.1M) need a dual-sourcing plan.
5. **Adopt the base scenario** as the target ($44.4M modeled, balanced execution risk),
   with aggressive levers phased in as capability matures.

## Strategic dispositions

Of 106 suppliers, the rule-based engine recommends
**REPLACE 12**, **NEGOTIATE 19**,
**CONSOLIDATE 50**, **RETAIN 9**,
**MONITOR 16** — a concrete action for every supplier.

*All figures are modeled opportunity from a simulated dataset, intended to
demonstrate the methodology and quantify potential, not to assert booked savings.*
